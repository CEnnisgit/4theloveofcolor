# Services Subpage Strategy & Blueprint

## 1. Legacy Audit & Key Inspiration
* **Legacy Copy/Vibe**: "Painting that improves how your home and business live."
* **Core Strengths to Retain**:
  * Deep-dive breakdown into the 4 Macro Services: Interior Painting, Exterior Painting, Cabinet Refinishing, Commercial Painting.
  * Explicit specification of surface prep standards (washing, scraping, caulking, priming).
  * Highlighting top-tier paint products (Sherwin-Williams Emerald®, Duration®, Gallery Series™).
* **Modern Upgrades**:
  * Rich service comparison & specification cards (`Card`, `Badge`).
  * Process step breakdown for each service category (`Accordion`).
  * Direct CTAs on each macro service card linking to the quote request form (`Button`).

---

## 2. Shadcn UI Component Matrix
* **Card**: Base container for macro service deep-dives and surface prep specs.
* **Badge**: Highlight key features (`Weather-Rated`, `Low-VOC`, `Spray Finish`, `Commercial HOA`).
* **Accordion**: Step-by-step prep and execution walkthrough for each service type.
* **Button**: Direct quote request trigger per service.
* **Aspect Ratio**: High-resolution showcase images for each service.

---

## 3. Design Philosophy
* **Visual Rhythm**: High-contrast, clean editorial layout alternating between `bg-warm-bg` canvas and `bg-white` service cards with 2px borders (`border-ink/10`).
* **Pill/Radius Discipline**: Strict `--radius: 0.125rem` (2px) corners on cards, badges, accordions, and buttons.
* **SEO Focus**: Targeted local keywords (Interior Painting Lakewood Ranch, Exterior Coating Sarasota, Cabinet Refinishing Bradenton).

---

## 4. Story Beat Map
1. **Beat 1 (Hero)**: H1 "Macro & Specialized Painting Services" + lead paragraph emphasizing Suncoast durability.
2. **Beat 2 (Macro Service Deep-Dives)**: 4 detailed sections for Interior, Exterior, Cabinet Refinishing, and Commercial.
3. **Beat 3 (Surface Prep & Paint Quality Guarantee)**: Callout grid explaining paint systems (Sherwin-Williams Emerald, Benjamin Moore Aura) and prep steps.
4. **Beat 4 (Call to Action)**: "Ready to transform your home or business?" -> Link to `/contact`.
