# Copywriting Audit (Legacy Website)

To ensure the new website retains the authentic voice of the original, we will reuse the exact slogans and headings crafted for the business. This copy focuses heavily on craftsmanship, local climate resilience, and family trust.

## Hero Section (The Hook)
*   **Eyebrow:** "Painters in Lakewood Ranch & Sarasota, FL"
*   **H1 (Main Slogan):** "Color, finish, and detail work that make a home feel intentionally renewed."
*   **Lead Paragraph:** "Family-owned interior and exterior painting for homes and businesses across the Suncoast — designed to sharpen every space, protect against the Florida sun, and deliver a cleaner result from the first walkthrough to the last detail."

## Section Headlines (The Narrative Beats)
*   **Services:** "Painting that improves how your home and business live."
*   **Portfolio (Work):** "Homes and spaces painted with care, skill, and a family's pride in the work."
*   **Why Choose Us:** "A more personal painting experience — built for the Gulf Coast."
*   **Process:** "From first call to final walkthrough."
*   **Promises/Guarantees:** "What you can hold us to — in writing, on every job."
*   **Testimonials:** "Real care, on every job."
*   **Service Area:** "Proudly serving the Suncoast."
*   **FAQs:** "Answers before you call."
*   **Final CTA:** "Get a free estimate for your next painting project."

## Key Terms & Vocabulary
*   **Locations:** "Suncoast", "Gulf Coast", "Lakewood Ranch villages".
*   **Tone:** "Intentionally renewed", "Sharpen every space", "Family's pride".
*   **Pain Points Addressed:** "Florida sun" (weather), "cleaner result" (mess), "in writing" (trust/guarantees).

**Execution Rule:** When building the Next.js frontend components, we will inject this exact copy into the `shadcn/ui` components rather than generating new generic lorem ipsum.
