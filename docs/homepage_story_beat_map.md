# Homepage Story Beat Map

The best landing pages do not just dump information; they guide the visitor through a psychological narrative. Based on the **Business Domain** (Family-owned, premium, reliable Florida painters) and the **Target Audience** (Homeowners tired of messy, cheap contractors), here is how the current wireframe maps to a high-converting story arc.

---

## Beat 1: The Hook (The Hero Section)
* **Wireframe Section:** 2. Hero Section
* **Narrative Goal:** Immediately answer "What do you do, where do you do it, and why should I care?"
* **Evaluation:** *Strong.* The layout correctly pairs a strong, benefit-driven headline (e.g., "Florida painting that actually lasts") with visual proof (the large, optimized exterior home photo). The immediate CTA ("Get a free estimate") captures high-intent buyers immediately.

## Beat 2: Establishing Authority (The Trust Strip)
* **Wireframe Section:** 3. Trust Strip
* **Narrative Goal:** Lower the visitor's defenses before asking them to scroll further.
* **Evaluation:** *Essential.* Highlighting "Family-Owned," "Fully Insured," and "Eco-Friendly" right below the fold immediately separates the business from run-of-the-mill, fly-by-night subcontractors.

## Beat 3: The Guide's Capabilities (Services Overview)
* **Wireframe Section:** 4. Services Silos
* **Narrative Goal:** The visitor asks, "Can you actually solve my specific problem?"
* **Evaluation:** *Clear and Concise.* Instead of a massive text wall, splitting into Residential vs. Commercial paths allows visitors to self-segment and find exactly what they need quickly.

## Beat 4: Visual Proof (Selected Work)
* **Wireframe Section:** 5. Selected Work (Showcase)
* **Narrative Goal:** Show, don't tell. Humans process images 60,000x faster than text.
* **Evaluation:** *Perfect.* Moving the portfolio up the page (before explaining the process) is a smart psychological trigger. Seeing the "Mediterranean Estate" or a clean "Bedroom Refresh" builds intense desire.

## Beat 5: Empathy & Differentiation (Why Choose Us & Guarantees)
* **Wireframe Sections:** 6. Empathy & Guarantees
* **Narrative Goal:** Validate the customer's pain points. (e.g., "We know you hate messy crews.")
* **Evaluation:** *Highly Persuasive.* This is where the story shines. Promising to "clean up every single day" and offering a "Walkthrough before final payment" directly attacks the anxiety of hiring a contractor.

## Beat 6: The Plan (Our Process)
* **Wireframe Section:** 7. Our Process
* **Narrative Goal:** Remove the fear of the unknown.
* **Evaluation:** *Reassuring.* A simple step process (Estimate -> Prep -> Paint -> Walkthrough) makes doing business feel easy and risk-free.

## Beat 7: Local Relevance (Service Area)
* **Wireframe Section:** 8. Service Area
* **Narrative Goal:** Confirm, "Yes, we work exactly where you live." (And farm local SEO).
* **Evaluation:** *Critical for SAB.* Listing Lakewood Ranch and specific villages proves local expertise and fuels the JSON-LD strategy we established.

## Beat 8: Social Proof (Testimonials)
* **Wireframe Section:** 9. Testimonials
* **Narrative Goal:** "Don't just take our word for it."
* **Evaluation:** *Necessary.* Real reviews validating the promises made in Beat 5.

## Beat 9: The Climax / Call to Action (Final Banner)
* **Wireframe Section:** 10. Final Call to Action
* **Narrative Goal:** The story is over. It's time for the user to act.
* **Evaluation:** *Standard & Effective.* A bold, contrasting banner at the bottom catches users who have scrolled the entire narrative arc and are now fully convinced.
